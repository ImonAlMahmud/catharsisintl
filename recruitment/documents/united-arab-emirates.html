United Arab Emirates Recruitment Framework

Embassy of Bangladesh, Abu Dhabi & Dubai Consulate Attested Documents

To facilitate seamless legal worker deployment from Bangladesh to the UAE, foreign recruiters and employers must provide the following three official documents attested by the Embassy of Bangladesh in Abu Dhabi or Consulate in Dubai.

1. Demand Letter

Employer Obligation
Download Sample Demand Letter

To make things easier for recruiters abroad, please send Demand Letters that indicate the desired employee types and categories, as well as the service conditions and remuneration packages specific to the United Arab Emirates.

2. Employment Agreement / Contract

Contractual Rights
Download Sample Contract

To ensure a smooth streamlined process, foreign recruiters must forward the employment agreement or contract delineating their responsibilities and the terms and conditions of employment.

3. Power of Attorney

Embassy Attestation
Download Sample Power of Attorney

A Power of Attorney, duly attested by the Bangladesh Embassy/Consulate is also required to facilitate visa processing for designated workers.
