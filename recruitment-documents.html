Standard Verification Workflow

4-Step Document Attestation Process

How foreign recruitment documents are verified by destination embassies and Bangladesh Ministry authorities prior to worker deployment.

01

Employer Drafts Documents

Foreign employer prepares Demand Letter, Employment Contract & Power of Attorney specifying trades, headcounts, and wages.

02

Chamber & Embassy Stamping

Documents are verified by Chamber of Commerce and attested by the Bangladesh Embassy / High Commission in the host country.

03

Ministry Permission (BMET)

Catharsis International submits attested papers to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare & BMET for official recruitment permission.

04

Trade Selection & Visa Stamping

Candidates are screened, medical cleared, and issued BMET Smart Cards for seamless flight deployment.
