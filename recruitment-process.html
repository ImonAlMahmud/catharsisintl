Phase 01: Pre-Employment & Candidate Sourcing

Demand analysis, vacancy broadcasting, trade testing & document dossier compiling
Steps 01 → 06
01

Requirement Received

Catharsis International receives the official demand letter from the foreign employer, specifying trade categories, headcount, salary packages, and service terms. Logged the same day.

Same-Day File Logging SLA: 24h
02

Requirement Analysis

Requirements are analyzed for trade clarity, salary corridor feasibility, medical standards, and visa headcount logistics. Clarifications raised within 48 hours.

Feasibility Assessment SLA: 48h
03

Vacancy Advertisement

The vacancy is published across our nationwide district-level sourcing network, Govt Data Bank, verified media channels, and registered district partner hubs.

Nationwide Broadcasting Districts: 64
04

Shortlisting Candidates

Candidates are shortlisted from the Govt Data Bank and direct applications, screened against experience, trade certificates, physical fitness, and document validity.

Strict Pre-Screening Pass Rate: High
05

Interview & Skills Evaluation

Employers interview candidates in person, via video link, or through our delegated expert panel. Accredited trade testing centres evaluate skills and issue scores.

Accredited Trade Testing Live & Remote
06

Document Submission

Selected candidates' complete dossiers — passport, trade certificates, police clearance, and educational credentials — are compiled and submitted for final approval.

Full Dossier Verification 100% Audit Ready

Phase 02: Legal, Contract & Medical Clearance

Bangla contract explanations, CMCL medical exams & Ministry visa endorsement
Steps 07 → 09
07

Employment Offer & Contract

Selected candidates receive official offers. The employment contract is thoroughly explained to the worker in Bangla before signing, ensuring transparent 2-year terms.

Explained in Bangla Ethical Standard
08

Medical Examination

Candidates undergo comprehensive medical & physical health screening at our own Catharsis Medical Centre Ltd. — ensuring 100% compliance with host-country standards.

In-House CMCL Clinic GCC Certified
09

Visa & Immigration Clearance

Catharsis International manages complete documentation, Power of Attorney, visa endorsement, and BMET immigration clearance under the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare.

BMET Smart Clearance Govt Approved

Phase 03: Deployment, Flight & Post-Arrival

Orientation briefings, flight ticketing by Catharsis Travels & post-arrival feedback
Steps 10 → 12
10

Pre-Departure Orientation (PDOT)

Candidates complete mandatory pre-departure orientation briefing covering destination country laws, airport procedure, workplace safety, and grievance channels.

Mandatory Training SLA: 1–2 Days
11

Flight Logistics & Deployment

Catharsis Travels Ltd. issues group air tickets, coordinates airport departure escort, and provides flight details to destination employers for airport pickup.

In-House Catharsis Travels SLA: 2–3 Days
12

Post-Arrival Check-in & Welfare

Our welfare officers confirm worker arrival, check accommodation conditions, and maintain ongoing contact to ensure worker satisfaction and employer feedback.

24/7 Welfare Support SLA: Ongoing
